## 📌 Project Overview

**Global Dub Plate** is a live audio marketplace and escrow platform built specifically for international reggae and dancehall sound system culture. Selectors, sound systems, radio DJs, and clash promoters worldwide can book artists currently "live in the studio" or "in dubplate session" to cut custom specials, station jingles, voice drop-ins, and metronome-synced tempo click acapellas.

### Key Features

1. **Live Studio Session Tracker:**
   - Real-time studio availability indicators (`🔴 LIVE IN STUDIO` vs `🟡 IN DUB SESSION`).
   - Filter by global territory: Jamaica 🇯🇲, Ghana 🇬🇭, Zimbabwe 🇿🇼, America 🇺🇸, Kenya 🇰🇪, Europe (UK/Germany) 🇬🇧 🇩🇪, Japan 🇯🇵.
   - Transparent price tiers for **Dubplate**, **Jingle**, **Drop-In**, and **Shout Out Acapella**.

2. **25% Deposit Escrow & Audio Preview Gate:**
   - Sound systems pay a **25% deposit upfront** to lock the studio session and vocal mic.
   - Artist voices the cut with the sound system's customized lyrics and uploads the track to the app.
   - **25% Audio Preview Gate:** The sound system selector can only audition and download the first 25% duration of the audio to verify voice authenticity, name pronunciation, and clash energy.
   - When reaching 25%, playback pauses with an audio tape-stop warning and watermark notice.
   - Full 100% master playback, high-definition 24-bit WAV download, split tracks, stems, and 4K studio video are released upon paying the remaining 75% balance.

3. **Artist Hit Catalogue Picker:**
   - Sound systems can choose from an artist's iconic catalogue songs (e.g. Buju Banton's "Champion" or "Destiny", Capleton's "Tour" or "Slew Dem", Chronixx's "Here Comes Trouble", Winky D's "Disappear", Stonebwoy's "Run Go", etc.) or request a custom/freestyle sound clash anthem.

4. **Custom Dub / Clash Riddim Selector:**
   - Choose to voice lyrics over legendary clash foundation riddims: *Sleng Teng, Real Rock, Stalag 17, The Answer, Bam Bam, Golden Hen, Showtime, Diwali, Punanny, Full Up*.

5. **Tempo Click Acapella (Zero Backing Music):**
   - For selectors who prefer raw vocals synced strictly on-beat to a metronome click (70–150 BPM) for custom sound clash remixing, juggling, and counteractions.

6. **Audio Delivery Formats:**
   - **Full Master Stereo:** Mastered 24-bit WAV file ready for huge speaker stacks.
   - **Split Track (L: Vocal / R: Riddim):** Classic sound clash standard allowing selectors to isolate vocals on the left channel and backing riddim on the right channel.
   - **Multitrack Stems Pack:** Separate stems for Lead Vocals, Backing Riddim, Dub Delay FX & Echoes, and Click Track.

7. **In-Studio 4K Video Capture Add-on:**
   - Selectors can tick a box to request studio video proof of the artist voicing in the booth, holding up a sound system slate for clash verification and Instagram/TikTok reels.

8. **Sound Clash Soundboard & Synthesizer:**
   - Web Audio API real-time sound effects: **Analog Dub Siren** (LFO pitch modulator + space tape delay echo), **Sound System Airhorn**, **Sound Clash Laser**, and **Turntable Tape Stop**.

9. **Artist Studio Portal & Global Registration:**
   - Dual interface for recording artists and studio engineers to receive orders, voice specials on mic, review escrow balances, and set custom rates.
   - Open registration for reggae/dancehall artists worldwide to join the platform roster.

10. **Comprehensive User Profile & Sound System Identity:**
    - Click **"👤 Selector Profile"** in the top navigation bar to open the comprehensive profile management console.
    - **User & Contact Information:**
      - Full Name, Email Address, Direct Phone / WhatsApp number for international studio coordination, City & Country.
    - **Sound System / Radio Identity (Editable):**
      - **1. Sound Name / Radio Name (Editable):** Official sound system or radio station name called out by the artist.
      - **2. Crew Members:** Lineup of selectors, MCs, dubbox operators, and engineers to big up in vocal intros and outros.
      - **3. Areas to Mention:** Localities, neighborhoods, or territories to represent in the lyrics (e.g. Johannesburg, Kensington, Harare, Kingston, Brixton, Brooklyn, Tokyo).
      - **4. Tag Line:** Official slogan or audio trademark (e.g. *"The Undisputed Dubplate Champions • Pure Reggae & Dancehall Pressure"*).
      - **5. Suggestions (Extra Info for Dub or Jingle):** Voice directions, pronunciation guidance, clash callouts, energy levels, and clean vs clash requirements sent to the artist's studio screen.
    - **Live Artist Studio Slate Preview:** Reactive real-time preview showing how the artist will read and voice the sound system's shout-outs.
    - **1-Click Autofill Integration:** Auto-applies profile defaults and sound system specifications directly into the dubplate order form.

11. **Modern 6-Track Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) & Auto-Hide Mixer:**
    - **6 Multitrack Synchronized Channels:** TR-808 Riddim, Juno 106 Lead Vocal, Moog Sub 37 Backing/Chants, Korg Minilogue Dub FX, Yamaha DX7 Echo/Reverb, Korg MS1 Clash Drops, plus Master Stereo Buss.
    - **Arrangement Timeline & Bar Grid:** Bar ruler (1 to 49), real-time vertical playhead scrubber, SMPTE timecode and bar/beat meters.
    - **Audio Editing Suite:** Selection pointer (`V`), scissors audio slice tool (`C`) with razor-blade splitting, audio fade/gain curve shaper (`E`), and boundary slip/trim (`T`).
    - **Auto-Hide Mixer Dock:** Smooth auto-collapsing 34px bottom mixer dock that expands on hover or click for full screen timeline real estate, with pinned open toggle.
    - **Dual LED VU Meters & Faders:** 12-segment dual LED VU meters displaying real-time RMS levels, pan rotary dials, dual delay sends, and long-throw channel faders.
    - **Slide-out Audio Pool & 24-bit WAV Mixdown:** Dedicated takes drawer with quick audition, track routing, and master bounce export.

12. **Native macOS Desktop Application & Standalone DMG Installer:**
    - **FL Studio Window Architecture:** Floating, draggable Mixer console (<kbd>F9</kbd>) over multitrack Playlist arrangement (<kbd>F5</kbd>).
    - **Zero-Latency Desktop Window:** Hardware-accelerated 60fps borderless DAW window with background audio throttling disabled.
    - **One-Click Drag-and-Drop Installation:** Packaged into `dist/Global-Dub-Plate-1.0.0.dmg` with `/Applications` symlink and Dubplate master vinyl app icon.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Node.js (v18+)
- macOS (for .dmg and .app bundle packaging)

### Running the Web Platform

Run the built-in HTTP server:
```bash
node server.js
```
Or with npm:
```bash
npm start
```

Visit the app in your browser: `http://localhost:3333`

### 💽 Building the macOS DMG Installer

To build the native macOS `.app` bundle and compressed Apple Disk Image (`.dmg`):
```bash
npm run build:dmg
```

The outputs are generated into `dist/`:
- `dist/Global-Dub-Plate-1.0.0.dmg` (1.3 MB compressed drag-and-drop installer)
- `dist/Global Dub Plate.app` (Standalone macOS Application)

To install:
1. Double click `dist/Global-Dub-Plate-1.0.0.dmg` to mount the disk image.
2. Drag `Global Dub Plate.app` into `/Applications`.
3. Launch from Launchpad or Spotlight.
