Sound System Culture & The Art of The Dubplate

In Jamaican and international sound system culture, a Dubplate (or "Special") is an exclusive, one-of-a-kind recording of a popular song where the artist re-records their lyrics specifically praising your Sound System and calling out rival sounds by name.

1. Dub Plate (Special)

Full vocal adaptation of an artist's anthem with customized lyrics killing the competition in a sound clash.

2. Jingle / Radio Drop

Radio station ID, mixtape opener, or podcast intro with high-energy sound effects, lasers, and sirens.

3. Drop-In / Name Drop

Quick 5 to 15 second voice signature dropped by the selector during live dances, club sets, or transitions.

4. Tempo Click Acapella

Dry vocal recorded strictly on beat to a metronome click (no backing music) so you can drop it over ANY riddim in a clash!

Why Split-Track & Stems Matter

Sound system selectors traditionally requested Split-Track Cuts: the vocal is recorded on the Left Channel, and the backing riddim is on the Right Channel. This allows the selector on a sound clash preamp to cut the bass, play the vocal raw, drop the riddim back in with echo delay, or swap riddims on the fly.

How the 25% Deposit Escrow Protects You & The Artist

1. 25% Deposit locks in the artist's studio time and vocal mic.
2. The artist voices your sound system name, records the cut, and uploads it.
3. 25% Audio Preview Gate: You audition the first 25% of the cut to verify voice authenticity, custom name pronunciation, and sound clash energy.
4. 100% Release: Pay the remaining 75% balance to unlock the full unwatermarked master WAV, split-track, stems pack, and in-studio 4K video proof!
